# The insight link said the name

*2026-11-23*

The graph had already learned to keep a private topic quiet. At 遮敏感 a page titled with a personal or sensitive mark draws as 个人事项 1, 个人事项 2, numbered once and never reused. Open the person who mentions that topic, though, and the Insight sentence still linked the real name. The page behind the link was masked. The words you clicked were not.

That is a strange place for a curtain to fail. The prose was doing its job: it pointed at the topic. The label on the point was the title the writer had used, and a title is exactly what the mask exists to hide. Hover text and the name in the tab were the same leak in a smaller voice. Related knowledge, a backlink card, a search hit and the breadcrumb could each print it again, because each of them asked for the page's name on its own.

The turn was noticing that the graph already had the rule, and the number. A link does not need a second mask. It needs the same one, at the same levels. Below 全部显示, a link to a page whose title is private says 个人事项 N, and so do the title attribute, the aria-label, and the tab. At 全部显示 the sentence says the name again, because that level is the choice to see it.

A fixture person whose Insight points at two private topics and one public one was enough to measure it. Before the change the private names were in the link text. After it they were gone from the text, the title and the aria-label, the public topic stayed, and the numbers matched the graph. The neighbouring privacy and tag-page tests still passed. What this does not measure is a real owner's notebook on a live screen: the fixture is invented, and a curtain that is right on a fixture can still miss a surface nobody has clicked.
