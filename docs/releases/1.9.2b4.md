## The second pass goes deeper

A page's second investigation reads its material in rounds, and each round may
ask for follow-up mail searches: the reply to a request, how two people met, a
name from a thread. On the 1.9.2b3 trial notebook (the owner's real mail,
2026-10-10) those searches did not do their job:

| Measured on the 1.9.2b3 trial | |
|---|---|
| Round investigations | 16 |
| Of which lost every follow-up search to Outlook HTTP 400 | 5 |
| Follow-up results on one round that were newsletters and digests | 19 of 19 |
| Final rounds that answered NO CHANGE | 6 of 17 |
| Person pages saying "not established" outside Uncertainties | 56 of 87 |
| `co rem logs --usage` | traceback |

- **Searches run.** Outlook wraps the query in quotes of its own, so the
  model's phrase quotes (`"pitch coaching"`) made every such query HTTP 400.
  Quotes are now stripped; the words still find the mail.
- **Answers are people.** A result from an automated sender (noreply,
  notification, newsletter, digest) is left out of what the model is handed.
- **One editing round, named by code.** After the final round, the lines that
  still say what is not known (`not established`, `is unknown`, `not
  confirmed`, …) outside Uncertainties and Sources are counted. If any remain,
  one more turn lists them by section and asks for each to be rewritten as
  what the material does establish, moved under Uncertainties (five at most)
  or deleted. The run record keeps `hedged_lines: [before, after]`, and the
  final-round note now says that NO CHANGE is only right when the page already
  reads as one account.
- **`co rem logs --usage`** reads a run whose instruction size was recorded as
  one number (156 skill runs on the trial notebook) instead of crashing.

## Verification and limits

- Each fix has a regression test that failed before it; the co rem unit suite
  passed (1,638 tests across the rem files) on this branch.
- The editing round is one turn, once per investigation. It fixes the lines it
  names; it does not re-read the material, so a hedge that is wrong because the
  reply was never searched stays until the next pass asks for that search.
- The automated-sender rule is the map's own (`AUTOMATED_HINT`): a calendar
  invitation or an `info@` reply is left out of search answers too.
- Not changed: how the nightly round chooses pages, the round cap, or the
  first run.
